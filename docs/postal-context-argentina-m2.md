# Argentina (AR) Postal Context M2 review

\nResult: **blocked / M2 unmet**

## Technical summary

Correo Argentino maintains the national CPA master and offers individual address lookup for the current eight-character `ANNNNAAA` code. An official 2023 AAIP record documents that individual epistolary lookup is free, while partial or total processing of the CPA database is a commercial product supplied through batch/Web Service and particular agreements with defined scope and intellectual-property protection. No complete rights-cleared current assignment/alias/validity/exception/non-area denominator, fixed postal Polygon/MultiPolygon product or compatible AGID public-serving right was found. M2 therefore remains blocked, and no raw source body, address, lookup response or geometry was committed.

## Country-specific M2 definition

AR reaches M2 only when a current complete Correo Argentino-authorized denominator of every `ANNNNAAA` assignment, alias, validity interval, correction, exception and typed urban block face, small settlement, rural zone, P.O. box, organization, other special or explicit non-area object is fixed with retrieval time, edition, stable row identity, schema, coverage, bytes and SHA-256 under rights compatible with AGID processing, storage, derivation, redistribution and public serving.

Individual lookup observations, a format pattern, historical UPU examples, the commercial batch/Web Service description and Georef address or territorial responses do not satisfy the denominator. Every drawable CPA must reconcile to a fixed real finite closed valid Polygon/MultiPolygon with postal authority, official/derived/virtual class, source, edition, reference date, CRS, topology, method, confidence and exceptions. A block-face label does not manufacture an area. Georef points or territorial entities, streets, address ranges, locality/administrative/census boundaries, parcels, buildings, buffers, interpolation, hulls, Voronoi/raster cells, AGID cells and synthetic fixtures are prohibited proxies.

Source-grounded route, P.O.-box, organization, Point or other non-area cases must expose the available geometry and reason without a fabricated surface; under the strict rollout criterion M2 remains unmet while no eligible postal Polygon/MultiPolygon exists. An approved immutable artifact must drive the real AR API/application path and all required states and provenance fields.

## Exact official evidence

Eight exact official bodies total 432,562 bytes and are byte/SHA-256 bound in `reports/postal-context-m2/ar-source-review-2026-08-31.json`.

- [Correo Argentino CPA](https://www.correoargentino.com.ar/encabezado/cpa) says the operator maintains and updates the national locality, street and height database.
- [Correo Argentino individual CPA search](https://www.correoargentino.com.ar/formularios/cpa) accepts an address and describes the complete eight-character code, including block, rural-zone and postal-box semantics.
- [Correo Argentino address normalization](https://www.correoargentino.com.ar/4-normalizacion-de-domicilios) offers CPA assignment through batch and Web Service.
- [AAIP EX-2023-59298590](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/sites/default/files/1334_-_4-7-2023_-_if-2023-76657116-apn-aaip.pdf) records a request for every Buenos Aires CPA. It records the operator's position that the database is an inseparable commercial product, individual epistolary lookup is free, and bulk territory queries or partial/total processing use particular agreements and protect intellectual property.
- [Georef v2 announcement](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/georef/nueva-version-de-la-api-georef) identifies an official open-source address and territorial normalizer. The fixed official OpenAPI `0.5.X` body has ten paths for territorial entities, streets, directions, reverse location and downloads, but no postal/CPA schema key and no postal-area endpoint.
- [CNRT Resolution 118/2011](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/resoluci%C3%B3n-118-2011-180387/texto) uses the Correo CPA database as an address-validation standard; it does not publish or license the database.
- The [UPU Argentina addressing sheet](https://www.upu.int/UPU/media/upu/PostalEntitiesFiles/addressingUnit/argEn.pdf) is dated `07/2002`. It corroborates the format and delivery examples but is historical, incomplete and contains no area geometry or AGID rights grant.

## Why M2 remains blocked

The public individual search is not a bulk reuse licence or a complete reproducible artifact. The official access record instead establishes commercial acquisition and particular-agreement gates for partial or total database processing. No contract, registration, authentication, payment or provider contact was attempted.

Georef is useful for address normalization and geographic context, but its reviewed contract has no CPA assignment field or postal area endpoint. A Georef point, locality, department, municipality, census unit or other territorial geometry cannot be relabelled as a CPA. Likewise a nominal urban block face is a postal object, not automatically a two-dimensional boundary. Zero official, derived or virtual postal Polygon/MultiPolygon records and zero production-eligible rows were validated.
