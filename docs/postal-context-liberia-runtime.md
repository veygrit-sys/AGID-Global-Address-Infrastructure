# Liberia Postal Context runtime

Liberia is modeled as a four-digit, locality-oriented postal-routing system with explicit assignment, geometry, address, building and privacy boundaries.

## Postcode and authority

The UPU Liberia addressing sheet dated August 2017 places four digits to the left of the locality name and shows separate street and P.O. Box examples. It establishes syntax and address order, not a current complete assignment table or canonical boundary coordinates.

The Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications is the government postal authority. Its 2025-2027 Service Delivery Charter describes postal services and regional offices, while the ministry publishes a National Digital Postal Address contract listing. These prove institutional, service-network and project context only. Production assignment still requires an exact ministry-authorized row or response with edition, digest, delivery type, observation time, validity and reuse rights.

## Geometry and administrative context

Production postcode geometry defaults to `none`. Office points, counties, districts, clans, census areas, parcels, buffers, Voronoi partitions, learned surfaces and AGID unions are not official postcode polygons.

LISGIS 2022 census geography may provide versioned administrative context only when an exact artifact, identifiers, CRS, digest, validity and licence are pinned. Census structure coordinates and household estimates are never public address or building records in this pack.

## Address, building and land

A source-linked postcode may resolve only its supported locality or post-office context. Street, house number, community, landmark, P.O. Box, recipient, telephone, entrance and unit remain separate evidence.

Exact building output requires a rights-cleared civic-address point and an explicit stable address-to-building relation. Liberia Land Authority parcels, deeds, interests and the developing Land Information System remain a controlled land layer and do not create postal or building authority.

## AGID, hosting and privacy

AGID is an independent spatial index and versioned crosswalk. It does not turn a cell, administrative boundary or model surface into postal evidence.

Only digest-pinned, rights-cleared, minimized and non-personal artifacts may be public on Cloudflare or Hugging Face. The ministry service charter promises customer privacy and confidentiality, and the reviewed 2026 draft Data Governance Policy says comprehensive data-protection legislation is still emerging. Recipients, senders, telephone numbers, residents, owners, land holders, correspondence, complaints and query histories stay excluded regardless of legislative stage.
