# Montenegro Postal Context runtime

The Montenegro pack keeps the five-digit Pošta Crne Gore destination post-office assignment, six-digit Postal Address Code (PAK), UZN address identity, cadastral buildings and parcels, and UZN/MONSTAT spatial units as distinct evidence. It never turns an office point, carrier route, address point, parcel, administrative boundary or nearest feature into an official postal area or exact building link.

Pošta Crne Gore five-digit destination post office
  -> six-digit PAK carrier street-part routing evidence
  -> separately permitted UZN address-register identity and house number
  -> source-defined or reviewed UZN cadastral building relation
  -> separately typed parcel, administration, statistical and privacy evidence
  -> ME AGID cell relation

## Five-digit postcode and six-digit PAK

The operator regulations make the five-digit Poštanski broj a mandatory address component and identify a unique post office. The branch directory also exposes office address, regional centre and delivery-office status. These are assignment and operational facts; an office point or delivery flag is not the perimeter it serves.

Pošta Crne Gore defines PAK as a six-digit code for the part of a street visited by a carrier. It improves routing precision but does not replace the five-digit postcode, prove a polygon, identify an exact building or describe a household or resident.

## Geometry is derived unless separately proven

No nationwide Pošta Crne Gore-authored postcode or PAK polygon distribution is assumed. Rights-cleared UZN address members joined to pinned operator evidence may produce a derived membership surface or route. Every output retains members, exclusions, method, uncertainty, validation, validity and lineage. Sparse, unmatched and ambiguous locations remain gaps.

Office buffers, nearest-office assignment, administrative or statistical boundaries, cadastral parcels, Voronoi cells and interpolation can be QA candidates only. They never become official Pošta Crne Gore postal geometry.

## UZN address and building precision

Montenegro law assigns UZN the house-number, street and square register and the spatial-unit record. Exact ingestion pins the authorized product, municipal coverage and completeness, edition, allowed fields, access or fee basis, terms, schema, source CRS and digest. The current system architecture confirms an Address Register exists, but that does not prove every municipality is complete or grant a public bulk licence.

Exact building output requires a permitted UZN building feature and a source-defined relationship, common authoritative cadastral identifier or reviewed explicit crosswalk. A house number, address point, parcel reference, property-list building number, text match, containment, overlap or proximity is candidate evidence only.

Real-estate and electronic property-list records are controlled validation evidence. Public artifacts exclude owners, rightsholders, personal identifiers, residents, occupants, title or right basis, encumbrances, restrictions, transactions, values, income and tax fields.

## Administration, licensing and runtime state

UZN spatial-unit graphics and MONSTAT names, codes and statistical hierarchy add dated municipality, settlement, local-community, cadastral-municipality and statistical-circle context. Current authority, vintage and coverage are pinned separately. Those units do not create postal membership, delivery eligibility, building linkage or sovereignty conclusions.

Viewer access, a search result, paid receipt or statutory access does not imply bulk or derivative redistribution. The committed seed is M1_metadata: contracts and non-production synthetic fixtures only. It contains no operator rows, UZN or MONSTAT features, real addresses, personal data or production geometry. Montenegro remains unconfigured until a separately released M2+ descriptor passes integrity, access, terms, fee, freshness, postal-evidence, derived-geometry, explicit-building-link, privacy, CRS, coverage and correction gates.
