# GS Postal Context

This country pack publishes the current official postcode `SIQQ 1ZZ` and real derived whole-territory display geometry for South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands.

The UPU assignment has no geometry authority. The displayed MultiPolygons come from fixed CC BY 4.0 BAS/South Georgia GIS coastline data and are not official postal, legal, cadastral, survey or delivery boundaries. No address, building, parcel, recipient, customer or land-right data is included.

See `M2-SOURCE-NOTICE.md`, `source-profile.json` and `docs/postal-context-south-georgia-south-sandwich-islands-m2.md` for source, rights, transform and validation evidence.
