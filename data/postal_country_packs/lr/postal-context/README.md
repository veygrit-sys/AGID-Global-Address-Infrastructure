# Liberia Postal Context seed

This directory is the metadata-only seed for the `agid-postal-lr` country repository.

Liberia uses a four-digit postcode written to the left of the locality name in the dated UPU addressing sheet. The sheet proves syntax and examples, not a current complete assignment register or boundary coordinates. The Ministry of Posts and Telecommunications is the current postal authority, but its public mandate, service charter, office list and National Digital Postal Address contract listing do not by themselves publish a reusable national postcode, civic-address or building database.

Postcode assignments, post-office facilities, counties and districts, census structures, land parcels, civic addresses, buildings, personal delivery records, ODbL candidates and AGID cells remain separate evidence layers. A locality, regional office, census enumeration area, parcel, nearest feature, model surface or AGID union never becomes a canonical postcode polygon or an exact address-linked building without explicit authority.

The included fixtures are synthetic conformance data only. They contain no current ministry rows, real addresses, production geometry, recipients, residents, owners, land interests, correspondence or query history and cannot satisfy a production promotion gate.
