Status: `M1 metadata / official operator and point inventories reviewed; complete assignments and areas missing`

The regulator lists three authorized public postal operators. Exact reviewed
JP BH Pošta and Pošte Srpske pages expose office/code point inventories, and
the BH PostExpress table exposes 468 office rows, but no reviewed source is a
current complete, versioned three-operator assignment release or postal-area
Polygon/MultiPolygon.

The UPU 04/2019 sheet confirms five digits and describes the first two digits
as a postal region and the last three as a post office. Those labels do not
define a full-code boundary. FGU/SDI and RUGIPP publish administrative,
cadastral, address and building context under different authority and reviewed
rights gates; none is promoted to postal geometry.

M2 requires a rights-cleared complete assignment, an exact authorized area or
a reproducible surface from complete permitted members, approved immutable
publication, and verification through the real BA loader, API and app. Search
must fit real geometry and draw a translucent fill plus clear outline while
showing authority, source, basis date and confidence. Raw source bodies stay
outside Git.
