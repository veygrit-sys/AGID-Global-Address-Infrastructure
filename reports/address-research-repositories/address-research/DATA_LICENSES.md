# Data And License Notes

This template is for research notes, metadata, and methodology. It is not a raw
address dataset and it is not a carrier integration repository.

## Allowed Material

- Public source metadata.
- Official-source URLs and citation notes.
- License summaries with links to authoritative terms.
- Synthetic fixtures created only for methodology examples.
- Redacted research summaries.

## Excluded Material

- Private address, recipient, witness, proof-secret, private-key, or production
  credential material.
- Raw carrier payloads.
- Proprietary datasets copied from source providers.
- Generated country-pack or postal-data releases.

## License Handling

Each imported note must carry one of these labels:

- `public_domain_or_open_license`
- `source_terms_required`
- `citation_only`
- `internal_review_needed`
- `do_not_publish`

Any item marked `internal_review_needed` or `do_not_publish` must remain out of
a public repository until reviewed.
